# Store screenshot captions

One caption per file in `docs/store/screenshots/` (1920×1080 PNG). Paste each into the caption box next to its
screenshot in Partner Center. Limit: 200 characters (Microsoft). Counts are Python `len()` on the exact text.

## 01-home-swipe.png (133 characters)

Each open public bid is scored for your trade, area and job size, with the reasons in plain words. Swipe right to save, left to pass.

## 02-home-scroll.png (116 characters)

Home: your best matches as a list, or one at a time. The pill at the top shows one government price index at a time.

## 03-bid-details.png (118 characters)

A bid's details and the reasons it fits. One tap opens the agency's own page: you bid there, directly with the agency.

## 04-search.png (66 characters)

Search: every open public bid that fits, with filters and sorting.

## 05-jobs.png (118 characters)

Example job shown. Local jobs: post work and receive sealed bids in the app. Amounts stay sealed until bidding closes.

## 06-job-awarded.png (102 characters)

Example job shown. When bidding closes the lowest bid wins, and the winner has 7 days to make contact.

## 07-conversation.png (128 characters)

Example job shown. After the award the poster and the winner can message in the app, and either one can report the conversation.

## 08-market.png (134 characters)

Market: monthly government price indexes for materials and wages for the trades, with their changes. Indexes, not prices for your job.

## How these were taken

- Shots 01 to 04 and 08: a fresh local account whose business name is "Your company" (Gardnerville, NV 89410,
  Electrical, 90 miles, jobs $10K to $50K), on a copy of the real 2026-11-14 scan data with the real BLS captures.
  Nothing in the page was edited. The days left are as of 2026-11-16, when they were taken.
- Shots 05 to 07: example accounts made for these pictures ("Alex (example)", "Example Electric",
  "Example Lighting Co.") and example jobs whose titles start "Example job:". There is no real customer data in
  them, and each caption says "Example job shown". To close the example job, the local server's clock was moved
  forward one day, so those screens read Nov 17.
- Desktop layout at 1280×720 CSS pixels and device scale 1.5 (03 at 1536×864 and scale 1.25, so more of the sheet
  fits; 01 at 1200×675 and scale 1.6 and 08 at 1600×900 and scale 1.2, re-taken later the same day so no card is cut
  at an edge). No logos, frames or marketing text were added.
