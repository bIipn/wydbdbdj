Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026

  • We keep what the app needs to find bids for your business: your sign-in, your business profile, what you do with bids, and the local jobs you post or bid on.
  • We do not sell your information, and the app has no ads and no ad or analytics trackers.
  • You can ask us to show you or delete everything we hold about you.

Who we are

BidSwipe shows open public bids (from public agency bid boards) that fit a contractor's trade, area and job size. You bid on the agency's own website; BidSwipe never takes or sees your bid on a public bid. BidSwipe also has local jobs: people post a job in the app and others place a sealed bid on it in the app. Questions about this policy: 3verettholt@gmail.com.

On your own device the app keeps a few small things in the browser's storage: an access code if your company's pilot uses one, and a promo code from a link until you finish signing up. Nothing there is sent anywhere else.

Like every website, our server sees your device's internet (IP) address with each request. We use it to slow down repeated wrong sign-in attempts and codes: the database keeps a short count of sign-in and sign-up attempts for each address and each email. A count stops counting within an hour and is deleted soon after. After you sign in, we remember that address together with your email for 30 days, so that someone else's wrong guesses do not lock you out where you usually sign in. Our hosting provider's request logs may keep the address for a short time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking scripts. The app loads its code only from our own server.
  • We do not see or store your prices on public bids. Those bids are sealed and go to the agency, not to us. (A bid on a local job is placed in the app, so we store it as described above.)
  • We do not ask for bank, card or tax ID numbers.
  • We do not track your location. Distances come from the home base you type in. If you tap “Use my location” while setting up, your browser asks you first; the app then sends your position, rounded to about a kilometre, once, to find the nearest ZIP. We do not store the position, only the ZIP you save.

Who else handles it

Only the services that run the app, and only to run it:

  • Hosting and database — Vercel hosts our server and Supabase stores the database.
  • Sign-in providers — if you choose "Continue with Google, Apple or Microsoft", that company tells us your email and an account id.
  • Push services — if you turn on alerts, the alert's title and short text pass through your browser maker's push service (for example Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft) to reach your device.
  • Email — if a daily bid email is set up for your company, it is sent through an email delivery service.

We would also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Change your business profile and alert settings any time in the app's settings.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in your browser or Windows settings.
  • To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account and everything tied to it (profile, saved bids, alerts, cost checks, devices, jobs, bids, messages, work profile), email 3verettholt@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We answer within 30 days. There is no delete button in the app yet: we do it by hand once we know the request came from you. We do not delete accounts on a schedule, so your data stays until you ask us to delete it.

Keeping it safe

Connections to BidSwipe are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and session cookies are stored only as one-way hashes. Every business sees only its own profile, lists and alerts.

Who may use BidSwipe

BidSwipe is for people 18 or older, the same rule as in our terms. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
