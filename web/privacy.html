Privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026

  • We keep what the app needs to find bids for your business: your sign-in, your business profile, what you do with bids, and the local jobs you post or bid on.
  • We do not sell your information, and the app has no ads and no ad or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete your account yourself, in the app's settings, at any time. What that deletes, and the little that is kept.

Who we are

BidSwipe shows open public bids (from public agency bid boards) that fit a contractor's trade, area and job size. You bid on the agency's own website; BidSwipe never takes or sees your bid on a public bid. BidSwipe also has local jobs: people post a job in the app and others place a sealed bid on it in the app. Questions about this policy: [CONTACT EMAIL — Ev to fill in before publishing].

Like every website, our server sees your device's internet (IP) address with each request. We use it to slow down repeated sign-in attempts, and our hosting provider's request logs may keep it for a short time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking scripts. The app loads its code only from our own server.
  • We do not see or store your prices on public bids. Those bids are sealed and go to the agency, not to us. (A bid on a local job is placed in the app, so we store it as described above.)
  • We do not ask for bank, card or tax ID numbers.
  • We do not track your location. Distances come from the home base you type in.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off in the app or in your browser or Windows settings.
  • Forgot your password? “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen emails you a link to choose a new one. Choosing it signs out every other device.
  • Delete your account yourself, in the app: see the next section.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings, then “Delete my account”. It shows what will end, asks for your password again, and then deletes the account at once. (An account that signs in with Google, Apple or Microsoft has no password: it signs in again and types its email address instead.) You do not need to write to us. It cannot be undone.

Locked out, or you forgot your password and reset by email is not available? Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with and we delete the account for you, the same way. We answer within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, password hash, the sign-in service's id, your plan and promo-code records, and any password reset link.
  • Every sign-in session, so every device is signed out, and every notification device.
  • Your business profile, fit scores, saved and passed bids, alerts and cost checks, and your work profile.
  • Every local job you posted, in any state: its title, description, address and phone, the bids on it and its messages. It also leaves every other person's feed, saved list and bid alerts.
  • Your own bids, messages and reports on other people's jobs.

What is kept, and why

  • Fee lines. A no-show or service fee that was recorded for you stays in our fee records, so their totals stay true: the amount and how it was worked out, the date, whether it was waived, the internal id of the job and of your account (which lead to nothing once the account is gone), the day you deleted the account, and the name on your account. Never your email address. Fees are recorded only; nothing is collected.
  • One line for people who were waiting on a job you posted. The winner of a job of yours that was awarded or agreed, and anyone with a sealed bid on a job of yours that was still open, keeps one line: the job's title, their own bid (and a fee that was waived for them, if there was one), the day it ended, and that it ended because the poster's account was deleted. Only they can see it. Nothing else about the job or about you.
  • A job you had won belongs to the person who posted it and stays theirs, without you: it shows that the winner deleted their account, and the winning amount. Your name, card and messages are gone from it.
  • What is already in other people's inboxes. An alert someone received earlier (for example “New message” with its first words, or the title of your job) stays in their inbox.
  • Backups and logs. Our hosting providers' backups and request logs can hold a copy until they are replaced [Ev: say how long your database and hosting providers keep backups and logs].

If a job was still running

  • A job you posted that someone had already won ends for them right away. They are told, they owe nothing, and a service fee that had been recorded for that job is waived.
  • A job you had won and not finished goes back to the person who posted it. They are told and can post it again. No fee is recorded because you deleted your account.
  • The terms say the same from the other side.

Keeping it safe

Connections to BidSwipe are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and session cookies are stored only as one-way hashes. Every business sees only its own profile, lists and alerts.

Children

BidSwipe is a tool for businesses and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.
