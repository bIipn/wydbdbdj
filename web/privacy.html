Privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026

  • We keep what the app needs to find bids for your business: your sign-in, your business profile and what you do with bids.
  • We do not sell your information, and the app has no ads and no ad or analytics trackers.
  • You can ask us to show you or delete everything we hold about you.

Who we are

BidSwipe shows open public bids (from public agency bid boards) that fit a contractor's trade, area and job size. You bid on the agency's own website; BidSwipe never takes or sees your bid. Questions about this policy: [CONTACT EMAIL — Ev to fill in before publishing].

On your own device the app keeps a few small things in the browser's storage: an access code if your company's pilot uses one, and a promo code from a link until you finish signing up. Nothing there is sent anywhere else.

Like every website, our server sees your device's internet (IP) address with each request. We use it to slow down repeated sign-in attempts, and our hosting provider's request logs may keep it for a short time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking scripts. The app loads its code only from our own server.
  • We do not see or store your bid prices. Bids are sealed and go to the agency, not to us.
  • We do not ask for bank, card or tax ID numbers.
  • We do not track your location. Distances come from the home base you type in.

Only the services that run the app, and only to run it:

We would also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Change your business profile and alert settings any time in the app's settings.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in your browser or Windows settings.
  • To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account and everything tied to it (profile, saved bids, alerts, cost checks, devices), email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with. We answer within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

Connections to BidSwipe are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and session cookies are stored only as one-way hashes. Every business sees only its own profile, lists and alerts.

Children

BidSwipe is a tool for businesses and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
