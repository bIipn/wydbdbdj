Terms for local jobs

Effective November 14, 2026

  • BidSwipe is where people post jobs and bid on them. We are not part of any job.
  • Bids are sealed until closing, and the lowest bid wins.
  • The winner has a set number of days (7 today) to make contact in BidSwipe. Fees are not being collected yet.
  • Licences and insurance are your own responsibility. We do not check them.

What BidSwipe is

BidSwipe is a place to meet. It is not a party to any job, does not hire anyone or do any work, and does not guarantee payment or the quality of work. The deal for a job is between the person who posts it and the person who wins it.

Public bids from government boards are different: you bid on the agency's own website, and these terms do not cover them.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and signed in. Anyone signed in may post a job and bid on other people's jobs, but never on their own.

How a job works

  • The poster picks how long the job is open for bids, up to 30 days.
  • Bids are sealed. While a job is open, only you see your amount. The poster sees only how many bids there are. You can change or withdraw your bid for free until closing.
  • At closing, the lowest bid wins automatically. If two bids are equal, the one placed first wins.
  • After closing, the poster and everyone who bid see the amounts, ranked. Only the poster sees who bid.
  • The winner has a contact window, shown on each job (7 days today), to make contact and work out the details with the poster. After the award, the winner sees the address and phone the poster added, and the two of them can message each other.
  • Making contact means any one of these in BidSwipe: the winner sends at least one message in the job's thread, the winner taps “We've agreed”, the poster taps “They contacted me”, or the poster taps “We've agreed”. A phone call alone does not count, but either side can record it with one of those taps. When one side taps “We've agreed”, the other side is told so they can tap it too.
  • An unanswered winner. If the winner's first message is sent with at least 48 hours of the contact window left (or half the window, if the window is shorter than 4 days), and the poster never answers by the deadline (no message, no “They contacted me”, no “We've agreed”), the job ends with the poster recorded as not answering. A first message sent later than that, or contact made only by a tap, ends with no agreement and nothing recorded on anyone.
  • A job that ends without a deal can be posted again.

Fees

The rates are settings. The figures on the bid screen when you bid are the ones that apply.

  • No-show fee: between 0% and 10% of the winning bid (5% today), owed by a winner who never makes contact within the contact window or gives the job back. Up to that share goes to the poster whose time was wasted (2% of the bid today), and BidSwipe keeps the rest.
  • Service fee: at most 3% of the winning bid (1% today), owed by the winner when both sides confirm they have agreed.
  • There is no fee when contact was made (as above) and the poster did not answer, when the two sides cannot agree, or when a job ends without a winner.

The exact figures are shown before you place a bid. Fees are not being collected yet. A no-show is recorded on your profile.

Licences and insurance

Many states require a contractor's licence for work above a small amount or work that needs a permit, and some trades always need one. Checking and following your state's rules is your own responsibility, and so is insurance. A licence or insurance shown on a profile is as the person typed it. BidSwipe does not verify it.

No promises about people or jobs

We do not check that a job, a poster or a bidder is what they say. Meet with care, and agree on the work and the price in writing before it starts.

Conduct

  • Post real jobs and bid amounts you mean.
  • No fake bids, harassment, discrimination, illegal work, or using a job to collect people's details.
  • Keep the other person's address and phone to yourself.

Reports and removal

Anyone can report a job from the job's screen. We may remove a job that breaks these terms, and it then disappears everywhere. We may also waive a fee. Posting, bidding and messages have limits to stop spam.
